Questions

Owners often ask

Which areas do you cover?

Cagayan de Oro and nearby towns. If your property is elsewhere in Mindanao, ask anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we can look after it well.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the property and how much you want us to do. After we've seen the place we'll quote a fee in writing. Asking and getting a quote cost nothing.

Do I have to live in Cagayan de Oro?

No. Many owners live elsewhere or abroad. That's usually why they want someone local to look after the place.

Who decides the rent and who moves in?

You do. We suggest a price and tell you what we know about each applicant, and you have the final say.

Can I still use my unit sometimes?

For short-stay units, yes. Tell us the dates and we'll block them on the calendar. For long-term rentals it depends on the lease.

Can you just list it, and I'll handle tenants myself?

Yes. We can put it on this site with your own contact details, and people message you directly. Say so in the form.
