Living in or staying at one of our units? Report a repair or ask us anything. Your message goes straight to the CK Haven team.

Urgent problem?

A burst pipe, water coming in, no power in the unit, or you're locked out: call us now instead of sending a message.

Call us

For fire, a medical emergency or a crime in progress, call 911 first, then let us know.

Good to know

Paying rent

Your lease says how much is due and when. If you need the payment details again, or a receipt, ask us with the form.

Moving out

Tell us as early as you can, and check your lease for the notice period. We'll arrange a walk-through and return your deposit as the lease says.

Guests on short stays

Your check-in details and house rules are in your booking messages. For anything during your stay, message us here or in the booking app.

Pets

It depends on the unit. Each unit's page lists its pet rule. Ask us before bringing a pet in.
